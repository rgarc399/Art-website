About

Frankie Pooh is a multidisciplinary artist working across painting, mixed media, and digital art.

Based in [Your City], Frankie's work explores themes of identity, emotion, and the intersection of the physical and digital worlds. Each piece is a conversation between materials — layered, textured, and deeply personal.

Available for commissions, collaborations, and exhibitions. Originals and limited-edition prints available through the shop.
