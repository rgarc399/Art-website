About

I'm Frankie Garcia — known in the creative world as FrankiePooh — a Miami-born Urban Contemporary artist whose work is rooted in street life, fashion, and Hip Hop culture.

Growing up in Miami, art, music, and style were never separate things — the bold edge of streetwear and the visual language of Hip Hop became the foundation of how I create and how I see the world. I've been an artist my whole life, but I committed to this craft professionally four years ago with a clear vision: to build something that extends beyond myself.

That vision took on new meaning through my journey with cancer — a defining experience that renewed my sense of purpose and deepened my commitment to approach every canvas with intention and gratitude. My work reflects resilience, culture, and authentic expression. I welcome the opportunity to collaborate — reach out to discuss commissioning a piece.
